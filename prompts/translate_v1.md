dataset. Requirements:

- Faithful and literal where terminology matters: 安全、可靠、可控 = "safe, reliable, and

  价值对齐 = "value alignment".
- Preserve rhetorical register; do not smooth, summarize, or omit.
- Respond with ONLY the translation, no preamble.
